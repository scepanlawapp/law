# Assistant Contract Review

A read-only review of an uploaded or filed contract: key terms, risky clauses, missing clauses, and conflicts with mandatory Serbian law with legal-source citations. The review is stored, shown in an "Analiza" rail tab, and exportable as a DOCX memo.
