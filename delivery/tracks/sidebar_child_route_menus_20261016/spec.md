# Sidebar Child Route Menus

## Goal

Let users explicitly choose a child route when activating Finance, Reports, or another sidebar parent item instead of navigating implicitly or expanding the sidebar.

## Requirements

- Parent items open an accessible Spartan dropdown in both expanded and icon sidebar modes.
- Preserve role-filtered child routes and translated labels/icons.
- Mark the active parent and current child route.
- Opening a menu does not navigate or expand the sidebar.
- Choosing a child navigates to its route and closes the mobile sidebar.
- Preserve direct navigation for items without children and existing user-menu behavior.
